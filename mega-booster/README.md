# mega-booster

kRO's Mega Booster promotion (메가 부스터 프로모션, 2022 onward) for Ragnarok Offline. Renewal only. Sources: the kRO client (Dec 2025 build), gnjoy's 2026 Mega Booster guide (notice 278) and hazyforest.com; English item and sign text from ROenglishRE, the rest translated for this mod.

13 of the 22 Lv 80+ Booster Missions work; the other 9 need memorial dungeons this server doesn't have (see Deviations).

**Playing the Druid line?** Install the regional-items mod from Find Mods too. The English translation doesn't cover the Druid-only items, so without it their names and descriptions aren't in English. The items work either way.

## Deviations from kRO

- The Booster Box comes from the Booster Guide, once per account, for a character at Base Lv 10 or below. Setting `box_per_character` makes it once per character; `premium_start_box` gives the Premium box.
- The premium upgrade costs 50 Booster Coins (setting `premium_upgrade_coins`). kRO charges 150 Nyangvine Fruit, which has no source outside kRO's cash shop; setting it to 0 restores that.
- Booster Missions 2, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 21 and 22 are listed but can't be started: rAthena has no Old Glast Heim Hard or Challenge mode, no Fall of Glast Heim, Bio Lab Expedition, Geffen Night Arena, Tomb of Remorse or Constellation Tower scripts, its Illusion of Labyrinth quest is broken, and the app doesn't load its custom Geffen Magic Tournament.
- Illusion missions are checked from the stock member's 4 h standby quest, so report to Lime before talking to that member again after the standby ends. Mission 1 is the Hard version (18205) for Lv 130+, since rAthena's Half Moon picks Hard by the leader's level.
- rAthena has no Episode 19, so the EP 19 milestone and the currency exchange open by level (210 and 200).

- Booster Pack (1) gives Doram a Metal Foxtail and the Druid line a Metal Mace instead of kRO's Metal Dagger, which they can't equip.
- The Druid line (needs app 1.6.4 or newer) is the mod's own addition to the Job Agent: rAthena has no job-change quests for it.
- On an iRO client without a kRO fallback GRF, the Job Agent offers no Druid-line job changes. iRO's data.grf has no Druid, Karnos or Alitea sprites, skills or items, so a Druid there would be invisible; a kRO data.grf set as the fallback GRF supplies that art.
- Known issue: the Attacker Booster Manteau shows no icon or sprite. The art is in the client, but the app's asset server can't find it under the name roBrowser asks for (웤웤망토); the fix is in the asset server, not this mod.

Values with one source are marked `[unverified]` where they're used.

## What to check in game

1. Talk to the Booster Guide at izlude 127,237 (Doram: lasa_fild01 44,301) with a new character, open the box, use the Booster Call: Lime appears next to you for 60 s.
2. Start and report the Lv 1 Fabre hunt.
3. At Lv 100, the enchant menu opens the enchant window.
4. Upgrade with 50 Booster Coins (`@item 1000254 50`), then check the premium coin shop.
5. Job Agent: rebirth a Lv 99 second job, then change to the transcended 1st job; you should land at Base 60 / Job 50.
